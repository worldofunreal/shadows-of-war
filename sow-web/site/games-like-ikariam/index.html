What carries over from an island builder

01

Cities that are worth defending

Cities, factories, ports and towers sit on ground you claimed, so a building is a commitment to a border. Lose the land and you lose the investment — the same reason a pillaged island stings.

02

Sea reach without a separate map

Ports extend what your coastline can do. Water is not a decorative border; it is how a coastal empire projects force to somewhere it could not walk.

03

The neighbour problem

Ikariam's best moments are diplomatic: a non-aggression pact, a shared island, a quiet betrayal. Here the same choices happen in a live match where the map updates as you talk.

Differences

Where it departs from Ikariam

  • One map instead of many islands. Everyone is on the same contested space, so geography is shared information and every border is somebody's plan.
  • Real-time pressure. There is no "come back tomorrow" layer. If you stall, your neighbour expands into the gap.
  • Leaders, not civilisations with tech trees. A historical leader grants one bonus and that is your identity for the match. Meet the leaders.
  • Terrain punishes attackers. Defensible ground changes what an army is worth, which is a different kind of planning from unit counters.
  • A match ends. Most land wins, the map closes, a new war starts.

Start here

Coming from an island builder: what to do first

  1. Take the land around your start. Unclaimed tiles are free economy.
  2. Put your first buildings where they can be defended, not where they look tidy.
  3. Use a port to skip a march. Coastal reach is often faster than pushing through a fortified frontier.
  4. Trade information, not just resources. Knowing which neighbour is exposed is worth more than gold.

FAQ

Is there trading between players?
The economy is built on held land paying gold and troops and on buildings that convert that land. It is not a market-based trade simulator.
Can I turtle on my own island?
You can play defensively, but the win condition is held territory, so a purely passive game loses on score.
Does it need an account or download?
Neither. It runs in the browser; native mobile builds are in alpha.
