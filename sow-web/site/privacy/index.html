Privacy
policy.

Last updated: September 2026 · Controller: World of Unreal · Contact: hello@shadowsofwar.io

01

Overview & controller

Shadows of War is operated by World of Unreal. This policy covers shadowsofwar.io, partner platforms, and native clients. Privacy contact: hello@shadowsofwar.io. We have no EU/UK establishment — send all requests, including GDPR requests, directly there.

Partner-platform SDKs may collect data under their own policies. Card data is processed directly by the store you pay through (Google Play, Apple, Stripe); we never see card numbers.

02

Eligibility & children

13+. Players 13–17 need a parent or guardian's permission. The game is not directed to children under 13; under-13 accounts are suspended and erased. Parents: write to hello@shadowsofwar.io and we delete promptly.

03

Data we collect

  • Gameplay: display name, clan tag, leader/civilization choices, match inputs, history, ranks, owned items.
  • Account: random account ID, linked platform identities, one-way hash of your ownership secret (never the plaintext).
  • Technical: build version, platform/locale, connection metadata, relay tickets, server logs for security and anti-abuse.
  • Product analytics (first-party only): closed-taxonomy events (visits, boot stages, tutorial, matchmaking, match start/end) tied to a random session ID and account ID. No ad identifiers, no third-party vendor.
  • Purchases: product ID, transaction reference, entitlement status via RevenueCat/the store. Full payment details stay with the store.
  • Public by design: names, handles, rankings, and match records are visible to other players.
04

Use & legal bases

Running the game, security and anti-cheat, funnels and reliability, support, compliance. EU/UK bases: contract, legitimate interests (never behavioral advertising), consent where asked, legal obligation. We do not sell personal data and run no ad trackers.

05

Sharing

  • Hosting under our instructions: IONOS, Microsoft Azure, Cloudflare.
  • Payments: Google Play / Apple / Stripe via RevenueCat, under their policies.
  • Partner SDKs: under their own policy when you play through them.
  • Players & public: names, handles, ranks, match records.
  • Authorities & transfers: where required by law, or to a lawful successor with notice.
06

Cookies & local storage

Strictly-necessary storage only: theme preference, session IDs, our own first-party event endpoint. No advertising, social, or cross-site cookies. Fonts are self-hosted — zero cross-origin requests. Details and controls in our Cookie Notice.

07

Retention

Event files auto-prune past 90 days; analytics counters and memberships expire by key TTL within 90 days. Deploys never wipe live state. Accounts and history persist until you request deletion or termination under the Terms.

08

Your rights & deletion

Access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict. Email hello@shadowsofwar.io with your in-game account ID (or name + approximate creation date). Verified deletions erase the account, identity mappings, metadata mirror, and analytics memberships — confirmed back to you. GPC is honored as a sale/sharing opt-out (we don't sell or share, so nothing toggles). Appeals by reply; response within 45 days at most.

09

Security, transfers, changes

TLS in transit, loopback-only internals, bearer-gated operator endpoints, hashed ownership secrets; no absolute guarantee. Infra runs in the US and EU with data-minimization and no ad-tech transfers. Material policy changes take effect after prominent notice; continued use means acceptance.

10

Contact

Privacy: hello@shadowsofwar.io · Help: support center · Security flaws: privately to hello@, never in public — acknowledged within 48 hours.

Server-side components are AGPL-3.0-or-later; source on GitHub.
