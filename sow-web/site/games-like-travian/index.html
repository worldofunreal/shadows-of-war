Overlap

What Travian players are actually looking for

01

Neighbours who are people

The memorable part of any long-running server is not the build queue, it is the player two villages over who either keeps the peace or ruins your week. Shadows of War puts that relationship on a live map where fronts, not inboxes, decide the outcome.

02

Land as the engine

Held tiles pay gold and troops, and ground is what you spend on buildings. Expansion is not a chore between real decisions — expansion is the real decision, because the map is the scoreboard.

03

Alliances that can break

Pacts are informal. Nobody enforces them but the threat of the next push. That is exactly the social pressure that makes a map interesting, and it survives the move to real time.

Differences

How it differs from a classical browser empire builder

  • Tempo. No queue that finishes while you sleep. Army movement and frontier changes happen while you are playing, so decisions expire.
  • Space. One contiguous map instead of isolated villages on a grid. Your border touches someone who is also deciding what to do about you.
  • Ending. A match resolves. Most land wins, the map resets, the next war starts without a month of rebuilding.
  • Leaders instead of tribes. You pick a historical leader who grants exactly one bonus — attack, expansion, gold, troops or capacity — and build your opening around it. See the roster.
  • Terrain that matters. Defensive ground punishes attackers, so a frontier is a decision about shape, not just numbers.
  • No install. The game is a browser tab; Android and iOS builds are in alpha.

Start here

Your first hour, translated from Travian habits

  1. Pick the leader that matches your old playstyle. Raider: attack. Builder: gold. Land-grabber: expansion.
  2. Claim neutral tiles immediately. Free land pays; empty land pays nothing.
  3. Convert a stable core before you push. Cities, factories, ports and towers are what turn a claimed border into an economy.
  4. Watch the terrain before an attack. The cheap-looking push through bad ground is how armies disappear.
  5. Find one neighbour to talk to. Two players who agree on a border both get to spend troops elsewhere. Until one of them does not.

FAQ

Is Shadows of War a Travian clone?
No. It shares the genre — historical strategy, map control, alliances — but plays in real time on a single contested map instead of long server rounds of village building.
Are there villages and resource fields?
Land is abstracted into tiles that pay gold and troops, and buildings are placed to convert and defend that land. It is not a resource-field grid.
Can I play without registering?
Yes. Open the game, pick a leader and join a lobby.
Do I need to pay to compete?
No purchase is required to play a match.
