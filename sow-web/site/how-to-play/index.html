Shadows of War is a match-based MMORTS. Start with a commander, turn territory into an economy, forge temporary alliances, and fight for control of the map.

Getting started

Five moves
to understand
the match.

01

Join matchmaking & select match mode

Join the active matchmaking lobby. The queue cycles between Free-For-All (FFA), Team Battles, and Humans vs. World Nations across world maps including Europe, Asia, Africa, East Anglia, and the Americas.

02

Choose your commander & civilizational edge

Pick one of twelve historical commanders. Each leader provides a permanent mechanical multiplier—such as Julius Caesar's 1.10x combat strength, Cleopatra's +50% factory gold, Sun Tzu's 20% troop production rate, or Ragnar's +50% port gold. Compare leader traits

03

Claim territory & construct specialized districts

Expand outwards to capture adjacent tiles. Every captured tile scales baseline gold and troop income. Construct specialized buildings on your territory:

  • Cities: Increase max troop capacity and generate passive troop and gold income.
  • Factories: Generate gold revenue and increase troop recruitment speed.
  • Ports: Placed on coastal tiles to generate gold, troop income, and provide water access.
  • Bunkers: Fortify defensive frontiers, extend defensive coverage, and reduce incoming troop losses. City Armory modules increase maximum troop capacity.
04

Manage economy, terrain & army momentum

Conquest consumes troops. Reinvest gold from factories, cities, and ports to fund further construction. Use natural terrain multipliers (highlands and mountains increase attack defense cost) to anchor strong defensive frontiers while directing army momentum toward high-value targets.

05

Diplomacy, betrayal & map control victory

Form non-aggression alliances with neighboring players to coordinate fronts. A player secures victory by reaching the map control win threshold (controlling the required majority of total landmass) or by eliminating all rival commanders. Betrayal breaks an alliance immediately, turning shared borders into active combat frontiers.

Match structure

The server keeps one rolling matchmaking lobby and rotates the mode and map selection over time.

  • FFA (60% land victory)
  • Teams & Coalitions
  • Humans vs World Nations
  • Weighted world-map rotation
  • Real-time tick engine
