Getting started

Five moves
to understand
the match.

01

Join matchmaking & select match mode

Join the lobby. Modes rotate: Free-For-All, Teams, and Humans vs Nations, on maps like Europe, Asia, Africa, East Anglia and the Americas.

02

Choose your leader & bonus

Pick 1 of 12 leaders. Each gives one bonus, like Caesar's 10% attack, Cleopatra's +50% factory gold, Sun Tzu's 20% troop speed, or Ragnar's +50% port gold. Compare leader traits

03

Claim land & build districts

Take the tiles next to you. Each tile pays gold and troops. Build on your land:

  • Cities: Cities hold more troops and pay troops and gold.
  • Factories: Factories pay gold and refill troops faster.
  • Ports: Build ports on coasts for gold, troops and water tiles.
  • Bunkers: Bunkers cut your losses on defense. Armory modules in cities raise the troop cap.
04

Spend gold, hold hills

Taking land costs troops. Spend factory, city and port gold on more buildings. Hills and mountains cost attackers extra, so hold them and push where it pays.

05

Diplomacy, betrayal & map control victory

Ally with neighbors to hold a front. You win at map control win threshold (most of the land) or by wiping out every rival. Breaking an alliance turns that border into a fight at once.

Match structure

One lobby stays open. Mode and map rotate over time.

  • FFA (60% land victory)
  • Teams & Coalitions
  • Humans vs World Nations
  • Weighted world-map rotation
  • Real-time tick engine
