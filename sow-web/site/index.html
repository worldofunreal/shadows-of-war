Game systems

How matches work.

Land control wins, districts pay, terrain punishes attackers, modes rotate.

How do I start a match?

Open the game, choose a leader, and join an active lobby.

What happens during a match?

Take land, build cities, factories, ports and towers, ally, attack, and hold the map.

Which game modes are available?

The matchmaking queue rotates through Free-For-All, Teams, and Co-op on active maps.

Do I need an account?

No. You can join a match without registering.

What do leaders change?

Each gives one bonus: attack, expansion, gold, troops, or capacity.
