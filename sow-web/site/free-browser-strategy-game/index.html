What "browser strategy" actually means here

Not a mobile city builder in a browser wrapper

01

Real time, not a queue you check twice a day

Matches are live. Armies move while you watch, frontiers shift while you are deciding, and the map does not wait for a build timer to finish overnight. If you go quiet, your neighbours notice.

02

Territory you hold tile by tile

Land is the economy and the scoreboard. Held tiles pay gold and troops, and land is what converts into districts, factories, ports and towers. Whoever holds the most of the map when the match ends wins — so expansion is never busywork, it is the win condition.

03

Historical leaders with one clear bonus

Every leader grants a single edge: attack, expansion, gold, troops or capacity. Nothing hidden behind a rarity tier — you read the bonus and pick the opening you want to play. Browse the full roster.

Why play a strategy game in a browser

Five reasons the tab beats a client download

  • Instant start. No 2 GB patch before your first match. The client is already loaded from the site, so a lunch break is enough to play one.
  • Any machine. A Chromebook, a work laptop, a phone on the train. The same account-free flow works everywhere a browser runs.
  • Nothing to keep updated. The game loads the current build each visit, so there is no version mismatch with your friends.
  • No store gatekeeping. You do not need to install anything to find out whether the game is for you.
  • It still plays like a strategy game. Real-time movement, fog of decisions, alliances that break — the browser is the delivery method, not the depth limit.

First five minutes

How a first match goes

01

Open the game and pick a leader

The queue drops you into an active lobby on a map in rotation. Choose the bonus that fits your opening: gold for a fast economy, expansion for cheap land, troops or attack for an early push.

02

Claim land before you build

Neutral tiles are free momentum. Grabbing them first pays gold and troops and gives you a frontier to defend. Building before you have land is how a start stalls.

03

Turn land into an economy

Cities, factories, ports and towers convert held ground into production, reach and defence. Terrain punishes attackers, so the shape of your border decides whether a push is worth the troops.

04

Pick a neighbour to trust — briefly

Alliances are informal and unenforceable. Any pact can break the moment the map makes betrayal profitable, and that is the game, not a bug.

Questions

Free browser strategy game FAQ

Is Shadows of War really free?
Yes. You can open the game and join a match without paying or registering. If optional extras ever change that flow, the site will say so here.
Do I need an account?
No. You can join a match straight away.
Does it work on a phone?
The site and the client run in mobile browsers, and native Android and iOS builds are in alpha testing.
Is there a single-player campaign?
Humans vs Nations rotates through the matchmaking queue, so you can fight AI-led nations alongside other players. A separate story campaign is not part of the advertised feature set.
How long is a match?
Long enough for a real war, short enough that losing one is not a lost month. Exact pacing depends on the map and the queue.
Can I play with friends?
Teams mode rotates into the queue. The Discord is where players coordinate.
