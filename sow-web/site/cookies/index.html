01

Optional analytics on our website

We use site and game activity to improve Shadows of War. It stays off unless you allow it.

  • It counts visits, loading stages, menu actions, tutorial step progress, lobby joins, match starts and explicit exits, plus a broad screen class (mobile, tablet or desktop). It does not record names, chat, passwords, lobby IDs, purchases, map clicks or the full user-agent string.
  • Accepted events go directly to our first-party endpoint at /api/event. They do not pass through the game relay. Account IDs, when present, are changed on our server to a keyed, website-only pseudonym before storage.
02

Browser storage and retention

We remember your accept/reject choice in local storage. Only after acceptance, a temporary session ID and a small retry queue use session storage. Analytics also includes the build, language and a broad screen class (mobile, tablet or desktop), not a full user-agent string. Event files and memberships expire within 90 days. Verified account deletion removes linked event rows and account memberships; non-identifying daily totals expire within the same 90 days. Game settings, account continuity, crash recovery and cached assets use separate storage needed for those features.

03

Other game platforms

Poki, CrazyGames and Jest may measure use through their own SDKs under their platform terms. We do not copy those player-level events to our server. Android first-party analytics is disabled until its store disclosure is updated.

04

Your controls

You can accept or reject optional analytics with equal ease. Change your choice in the game’s Settings or here; rejecting does not affect gameplay. Clearing site data also removes the choice, and analytics stays off until you explicitly accept again. We do not sell or share analytics for advertising.
