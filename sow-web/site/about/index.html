About Shadows of War

Shadows of War is a free real-time multiplayer strategy game that runs in a browser. You pick a historical leader, claim territory on a contested map, build an economy on that ground, and fight other players for the rest of it.

Play free → Contact the team

The game

What it is, in one paragraph

A match starts on a map with neutral ground around you. Held tiles pay gold and troops, land converts into districts, and cities, factories, ports and towers turn a frontier into an economy. Terrain penalises attackers, alliances are unenforceable, and the player or team holding the most land when the match ends wins. The point is to make the last third of a war a real decision instead of a foregone conclusion.

The game is free to play, needs no download, and does not require an account to join a match. It runs in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Native Android and iOS builds are in alpha testing, which means they can change shape between releases.

What is in the current build

  • Real-time multiplayer matches through the matchmaking queue.
  • Free-For-All, Teams and Humans vs Nations rotating modes.
  • A historical leader roster that keeps growing — each leader grants exactly one bonus: attack, expansion, gold, troops or capacity.
  • World and regional maps in rotation.
  • Terrain that changes the cost of an attack.
  • This website in 15 languages, with the game selecting its own interface language separately.

What it is not

  • Not a pay-gated or login-only experience: there is no wall in front of the pages here.
  • Not a single-player story campaign, and not a licensed historical simulation.
  • Not affiliated with any other strategy game you may have played. Leader names refer to historical figures; comparisons to other titles are editorial, not endorsements.
