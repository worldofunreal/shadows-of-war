# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest version deployed to production is supported.

## Report a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Email hello@shadowsofwar.io. If you want to encrypt
your report, email us to arrange a secure key exchange.

We will acknowledge reports within 48 hours and provide an estimated fix
timeline.

## Scope

Reports involving account access, player data, purchases, multiplayer integrity,
service availability, or server-side input handling are in scope. Cosmetic
issues and minor rate limits are low priority; major service-disruption risks
are in scope.
