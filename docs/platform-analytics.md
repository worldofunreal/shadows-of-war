## Churn and conversion signals

- Tutorial start, individual step completion, and explicit early exit show where an engaged player stops. Closing a tab is not observable as an intentional churn event.
- Queue start, join, cancellation, wait bucket, and fixed failure category expose matchmaking friction without storing raw server errors.
- Match start, result, mode, and duration bucket distinguish conversion to play from completed play. `match_exit` counts only explicit menu exits before a result.
- Website reports include event counts and distinct sessions per UTC day. They do not calculate cross-session retention or unique people, and they do not reconstruct an individual journey. Historical schema 2 cohorts are labeled separately and expire after 90 days.
- Portal dashboards remain separate. We cannot claim cross-portal unique users, cross-device people, player demographics, or a single cross-platform retention rate.
