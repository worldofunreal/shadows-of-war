## Claims policy

- Say **browser build** and **play now**.
- Do not claim Steam, Epic, Poki, CrazyGames, or another partner listing is live
  until it is verified on that platform.
- Do not describe the matchmaking queue as all-human; the current server can
  fill matches with internal bots.
- Do not use generic “indie game” as the main category.
